# Cyber Incident Case Files
This directory is a record of cybersecurity incidents I encountered, investigations, suspicious events and how I did my best to make the impact minimum while dealing with real-world threats. 
Each case documents what happened and how, what I observed, the step by step procedure of how I responded, and what lessons I learned. Each case is documented not only to understand what happened, but also to learn how I could respond quickly and better in the future.
## Why I keep these Files
The one of the first real-incident I encountered about having two malware worms in my system and how I responded with chatgpt's divine guidance and my own cybersecurity learning made me realize how much it is required for me to document what I learned from a real-practical exposure to a real cyber incident. 
Cybersecurity is not only about completing labs and earning certificates. it is also about learning to investigate the unexpected.
